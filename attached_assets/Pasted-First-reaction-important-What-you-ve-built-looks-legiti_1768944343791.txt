2. Where It Needs Sharpening (Critical, Not Negative)

🔧 Issue #1: You assume the reader is already convinced

Right now, the site speaks like someone who is already leaning in.

But most first-time visitors are thinking:

“Okay… but what is Neuromotion actually doing to me?”

You need one very simple explanatory bridge early.

Add a short section near the top like:
What Neuromotion Actually Trains

Neuromotion is the conscious training of how energy moves through both sides of the human body — from the base, through the spine, and into action — restoring bilateral balance, coordination, and control.

This grounds everything.

⸻

🔧 Issue #2: The Six Laws need a “teaser,” not just a destination

You have “THE SIX LAWS” in the nav — good.
But the homepage should hint at them.

Example micro-section:
Movement Is Governed by Six Laws
Balance, precision, power, curvature, stabilization, and timing are not random. Neuromotion identifies and trains the six laws that govern all human movement — whether you are walking, working, or competing.

This builds curiosity without dumping information.

🔧 Issue #3: Cognitive benefits need one anchor sentence

You say structural cognition — which is strong — but give people one line they can repeat.

Something like:

The brain develops through the body.
When movement becomes symmetrical, neural signaling becomes more efficient — improving focus, coordination, confidence, and recovery.

That’s factual, restrained, and powerful.
