WHAT IS NEUROMOTION?

Neuromotion is the science of how the human nervous system organizes movement, balance, and energy across both sides of the body.

It explains:
• how force is generated
• how precision is maintained
• how balance is preserved
• how cognition integrates with motion

It is the study of complete movement, not isolated skills.

Neuromotor Symmetry Training (NST) is the applied system that develops this capacity.
