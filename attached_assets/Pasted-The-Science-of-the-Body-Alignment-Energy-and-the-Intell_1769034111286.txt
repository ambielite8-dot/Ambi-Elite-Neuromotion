The Body Was Never Broken

The human body is not fragile.

It is one of the most advanced self-regulating systems in nature.

It heals itself.
It adapts.
It strengthens.
It reorganizes.

The problem is not that the body fails.

The problem is that the body adapts to how it is used.

If movement is asymmetrical, the body adapts asymmetrically.
If load is uneven, the body redistributes load.
If one side dominates, the nervous system reinforces dominance.

This is not dysfunction.

This is intelligence.
