22. Humans don’t realize they have two muscle chains on each side of the body

This is one of the most overlooked truths in human movement.

Each side of the body is not a single unit — it contains two distinct muscular and neural chains:
	•	an Inner (precision) chain
	•	an Outer (power/protection) chain

On the dominant side, these chains are well developed and work together unconsciously through years of repetition.
But most people do not understand how energy transfers between them, or that energy must be contained between these chains to produce control.

On the non-dominant side:
	•	the chains exist
	•	the nerves exist
	•	the muscles exist

…but they are poorly coordinated, poorly sequenced, and poorly integrated.

Neuromotion does not invent this concept — science already supports it through:
	•	differentiated nerve pathways (median vs ulnar)
	•	fascial lines
	•	motor unit recruitment patterns
	•	bilateral neuromuscular organization

What Neuromotion does is organize this science into a usable system:
	•	the Inner Grip
	•	the Outer Grip
	•	and the laws that govern how energy must move between them

➡️ People finally understand why control exists on one side and not the other — and how to build it deliberately.
